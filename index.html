Load. Play. Explore.

Your existing browser engine, ready to play. No installation, wallet or compilation. The first launch downloads about 32 MB.

Desktop controls: W moves forward, S moves backward, A/D strafe, left/right arrows turn, Ctrl fires, Space opens doors, Esc opens the game menu. Click the game to focus.
